## Access limits

All 17 entries have a matching primary record or publisher-deposited metadata. Direct publisher pages and full texts were unavailable for some entries as marked above. In particular, the 1994 Shor publisher full text, the 1960 SIAM full text and the 1955 Wiley full text were not independently confirmed through their publisher sites. Their uses are limited to the recorded algorithm, code construction or historical attribution. Publication dates attached to working mirrors and DOI suffixes were not substituted for proceedings or publication years.
