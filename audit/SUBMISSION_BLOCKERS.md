## Conditional technical limits

- A full-verifier false-accept proof would be required if the paper were to restore a system-wide probability claim. The current scope uses a fixed-order sign-fit count and a separate ideal raw-score null. It reports observed verdicts for the actual verifier.
- The fresh liboqs-backed checks cover code behavior and release verification. The historical model comparison still uses recorded outcomes because the research key is absent. A venue-level reproducibility statement should disclose that distinction.
- The Shor, Reed–Solomon and Kuhn entries have primary publisher-deposited records. Full publisher text could not be confirmed for those editions during this pass. Their cited uses are narrow and the access limits are documented. [Reference verification](REFERENCE_VERIFICATION.md).
