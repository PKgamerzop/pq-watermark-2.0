## Figure 8A provenance

The historical “Without alignment” series used recorded Phase 6 outcomes for `permute_out_carrier`, `permute_in_carrier`, and `permute_all`. Those bars came from a historical verifier revision. The following bars substituted the fraction with zero recorded raw sign changes for the disabled decode rate:

| Configuration | Historical inferred disabled value | Recorded RS32 exact results with retries available |
|---|---:|---:|
| `scale_in_carrier_x10` | 0/9 (0%) | 9/9 |
| `scale_in_carrier_x100` | 0/9 (0%) | 9/9 |
| `permute_scale_in_x10` | 0/9 (0%) | 9/9 |

The historical “With alignment” series came from saved Phase 6b verdicts. The replacement panel uses only saved raw-sign-change measurements and recorded verdicts. The 108 permutation or combined-edit cases plus 81 pure input-scale cases all verified exactly with unchanged recorded accuracy. The separate 81 output-side rescaling cases all verified, with absolute recorded accuracy changes of at most 0.07 points. The format of the saved verifier JSON omits the selected candidate, so the revised panel gives the recorded result with retries available.

## Other edits

- `paper1/evaluation.tex` separates five paired clean runs from three robustness parents per design, their repeated modifications, and their controls. It corrects RS16's minimum absolute logit to 2.70 and uses matching seed denominators for clean comparisons. Carrier norms are descriptive. The naive benchmark uses 36 common configurations with equal configuration weight, and its method and sampling differences are stated.
- `paper1/render_audit_figures.py` regenerates five manuscript figures. Fig. 1 shows the trusted workflow. Fig. 3 plots carrier norms and energy measured from nine saved parent checkpoints instead of an unavailable pooled-logit reconstruction and causal annotation. Fig. 6 shows per-seed verdicts and mean accuracy for quantization. Fig. 7 shows the observed RS32 control scores. Fig. 8 uses the recorded data described above. Figures 2, 4 and 5 retain their original files with corrected captions where needed.
- `paper1/evaluation.tex` and `paper1/method.tex` shorten or resize table and equation content to fit IEEEtran column widths. The related-work wording distinguishes Dilithium benchmarks from the standardized signing parameter set and scopes the paper's contribution to integration and measurement.
- `README.md`, `docs/threat_model.md`, and `docs/phase7_results.md` align the public descriptions with the revised evidence. `experiments/phase7/robustness_report.py` changes reporting text and chart labels only, including the label for agreement with clean signs. `experiments/phase7/paper_figures.py` labels its renderer as historical. The saved data and graphics under `results/` and `runs/`, the release, and the verifier, attacker, training and embedding implementations are preserved. Historical plots under `results/` retain their original bytes and are identified as historical.
