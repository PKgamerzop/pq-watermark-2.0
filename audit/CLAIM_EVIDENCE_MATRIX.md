Repeated claims in the abstract, introduction, captions and conclusion share IDs.

- **VERIFIED BY REPOSITORY** means code, signed artifacts, saved result rows or independently checked arithmetic support the claim at its stated scope.
- **NEEDS REPRODUCTION** means the reported outcome requires a rerun, missing numerical artifact or additional controls to establish the claimed result.
- **NEEDS EXTERNAL VERIFICATION** means a primary source or wider literature check is needed to establish the claim at its stated scope.
- **UNSUPPORTED** means the evidence contradicts the claim or the argument does not establish it as written.

The audit independently recalculated saved aggregates. The empirical labels apply to the checked records and calculations. Fresh training and a fresh keyed verification require a separate run.
