\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

Research relevant to model provenance with post-quantum signatures spans three intersecting directions: post-quantum authentication of ML artifacts and deployments, post-quantum media and generative-output watermarking, and white-box neural parameter watermarking under structural attacks. Table~\ref{tab:related-work} summarizes how representative published works compare with our protocol.

\subsection{Post-Quantum Authentication and AI Supply-Chain Provenance}
Li \emph{et al.}~\cite{li2024federated} evaluated post-quantum signature schemes---CRYSTALS-Dilithium (the basis for ML-DSA), FALCON, and SPHINCS+---for authenticating model updates in federated learning, reporting the shortest overall simulation times for Dilithium among the three tested schemes. Stalin and Rai~\cite{stalin2025automotive} benchmarked Dilithium on automotive-grade hardware (an Infineon AURIX TC-222 microcontroller and an ARM Cortex-A53 SoC), documenting concrete key and signature footprints (1,952-byte public keys and 3,309-byte signatures for the reported Dilithium3 configuration) and showing that platform optimizations such as ARM NEON accelerate signing and verification substantially while bulk payload signing remains costly on constrained microcontrollers. At the supply-chain governance level, Campbell~\cite{campbell2026mbom} discussed the harvest-now, forge-later (HNFL) signature threat for long-lived AI artifacts and proposed a Model Bill of Materials with post-quantum attestation (MBOM-PQC) using ML-DSA and SLH-DSA. These studies address signatures on external release records or training updates. Our experiments examine whether an auditor can associate modified classifier weights with a signed manifest.

\subsection{Post-Quantum Watermarking for Media and Generative Outputs}
Several recent studies pair post-quantum primitives with watermarking for digital media or generative outputs rather than classifier weights. Hu \emph{et al.}~\cite{hu2026diffusion} embedded error-corrected watermarks in the latent space of diffusion models while protecting key material via post-quantum key encapsulation, demonstrating robust bit recovery from generated images. Ikram \emph{et al.}~\cite{ikram2026medical} designed a deliberately fragile, quantum-inspired DCT watermarking framework for medical imagery using SHA3-256 key derivation and coefficient entanglement to localize subtle pixel tampering---a tamper-evidence objective opposite to robust provenance retention. YunusBasha and UlagaPriya~\cite{yunusbasha2026deepfake} combined DWT--DCT video-frame watermarking, deepfake classifiers (CNN, EfficientNet, GRU), and simulated PQC/blockchain components for media authenticity, and reported classifier accuracy and latency. In the generative-AI patent literature, Sehrawat~\cite{sehrawat2026patent} proposed multiparty post-quantum verifiable watermarking using distributed key generation and biased token sampling, with trusted execution environments in some embodiments. In our setting the auditor reads classifier weights to assess their association with the manifest. Generated samples are unnecessary for extraction.

\subsection{White-Box Weight Watermarking and Structural Obfuscation}
Uchida \emph{et al.}~\cite{uchida2017embedding} introduced parameter-regularization watermarking, projecting averaged convolutional filter weights through a secret matrix and optimizing a binary cross-entropy penalty alongside task loss. Uchida et al. evaluated recovery under selected fine-tuning and pruning conditions. In a separate study of neural structural obfuscation, Yan \emph{et al.}~\cite{yan2023rethinking} studied function-preserving obfuscations using dummy-neuron insertion, permutation and scaling. Our work builds on Uchida-style projection embedding~\cite{uchida2017embedding} and Reed--Solomon coding~\cite{reed1960polynomial}, extends it with an ML-DSA-65 signed dataset manifest, evaluates extraction retries for selected channel permutations and positive scalings, and compares clean accuracy and overwrite outcomes for BCE and hinge objectives.
